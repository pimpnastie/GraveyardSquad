War Schedule

Each River Race runs on a weekly cycle: three training days to build your deck and collect medals, followed by four war days (Thursday–Sunday) where every battle counts toward the clan's fame total. Use all 4 of your war decks each war day — missed decks show up on the roster's slacker list and can lead to a strike after repeated misses (see the missed-war streak tracker on the dashboard).

Clan Culture

We're a competitive-but-friendly clan — push hard during war, help each other out with card advice and deck feedback, and keep things respectful in Discord. Leadership tracks participation and activity through this dashboard, not guesswork, so strikes and promotions are based on real data.
