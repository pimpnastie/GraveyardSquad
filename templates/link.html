Common Issues

My tag isn't found ▾
Double-check for lookalike characters in your tag, and copy it directly from your in-game profile to avoid typos.
I'm already linked to someone else ▾
Each Clash Royale tag can only be linked to one Discord account at a time. If this is your account and someone else linked it by mistake, ask an admin to unlink it via the Admin Panel.
Do I need admin approval? ▾
No — linking takes effect immediately once your tag is verified. There's no separate "pending approval" step for this clan.
